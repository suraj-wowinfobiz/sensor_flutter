# Privacy Policy for WowGuardian

**Effective date:** June 3, 2026

WowGuardian ("we," "our," or "us") provides a construction safety and sensor monitoring application for organizations managing worksites, sensors, devices, alerts, analytics, and related operational workflows.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the WowGuardian app may process, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

## Information We Collect

Depending on how your organization uses WowGuardian, the app may process:

- Account information such as name, email address, role, and organization details
- Authentication data such as login credentials and session tokens
- Operational data such as sites, devices, sensors, alerts, analytics, thresholds, and configuration details
- User-submitted business information related to construction site monitoring and administration

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Authenticate users and manage secure access
- Display dashboards, alerts, analytics, sensors, devices, and site information
- Support administration, configuration, and monitoring workflows
- Maintain app functionality, troubleshoot issues, and improve service reliability

## Data Storage and Security

WowGuardian may store limited session-related information on the device, such as authentication tokens and login preferences, to support sign-in and app usage.

We take reasonable steps to protect information during transmission and storage. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information through the app.

Information may be shared only:

- With your organization and authorized administrators
- With service providers or backend infrastructure used to operate the service
- When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security

## Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for business, operational, legal, and security purposes, or as required by the organization operating WowGuardian.

## Your Choices

Depending on your organization and applicable law, you may be able to:

- Request access to your personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of information where legally permitted
- Log out of the app and remove locally stored session data

## Children’s Privacy

WowGuardian is intended for business and organizational use and is not directed to children.

## Third-Party Services

The app may connect to organization-managed or service-managed backend systems to provide authentication, monitoring, analytics, and dashboard features.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at this location with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the organization or support team responsible for your WowGuardian deployment.
